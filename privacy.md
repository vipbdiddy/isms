# Isms Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Isms collects, how it is used, and who can see it.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address and a protected (hashed) version of your password.
- **What you add about your loved ones.** Their names, relationships, years, short descriptions, obituaries and photos, and the isms (quotes) and stories you save, along with the topics you tag them with.
- **Your saved favorites.** Which isms and stories you have saved (the bookmark).
- **Your name and friends.** The name you choose for other people to see (if you sign in with Google, it starts as your Google name), whether people may find you, whether your profile is public, and who you are friends with or have sent or received friend requests from.
- **Your groups.** The groups you make or join, who is in them, and the invitations you send, receive or decline.
- **Likes and comments.** What you like, the comments you write, and when.
- **Your contacts, only if you ask.** If you choose "Find friends from your contacts", the app asks your phone for permission, reads the email addresses in your contacts, and turns each one into a scrambled code (a SHA-256 hash) on your phone. Only those codes are sent, to check which belong to people on Isms; they are not stored. Your contacts' names, phone numbers and addresses are never read or uploaded. We only keep a count of how many times you checked each day, to stop misuse.
- **Reports.** If you report something (a memory or a comment), we keep the report and the reason you picked so a reviewer can look at it.
- **On your device only.** Your display preferences, and a name you enter before signing in, stay on your phone (or in your browser, on the web version).

## Who can see it

Everything you add is private by default: only you can see it.

For each loved one, you choose who can see their name, relationship, years, description, obituary, photo, isms and stories, and whether the people who can see them may add their own:

- **Just me** (the default): only you.
- **Friends**: you and the people you have added as friends in Isms.
- **A group**: you and the members of one group you choose, like your family.
- **Everyone**: everyone who uses Isms, including people who are not signed in.

You can keep individual isms or stories private, and you can change who can see a loved one at any time, which takes effect right away.

**Adding to someone else's loved one.** Unless the person who added a loved one turns it off, everyone who can see that loved one can add their own isms and stories to them. What you add is seen by everyone who can see the loved one, with your name, and the person who added the loved one can remove it. If they delete the loved one, everything added to them is deleted too.

**Groups.** The members of a group see who else is in it and who has been invited. When you leave a group or are removed from it, the loved ones you shared with it become private again (only you), and when a group is deleted, everything shared with it becomes private again for whoever shared it.

**Likes and comments.** Everyone who can see a loved one, ism or story can see who liked it and the comments on it, with the names of the people who wrote them. You can delete your own comments, and you can delete anyone's comments on the loved ones, isms and stories you shared.

**Your name** is shown to your friends, to the people in your groups, to the members of a group you're invited to, to people you send a friend request to or receive one from, and to people who search for it or have your email address in their contacts. It also shows with your comments and likes, to everyone who can see what you commented on or liked, and with the isms and stories you add to someone else's loved one, to everyone who can see that loved one. To stop people finding you by name or email address, turn off **Settings (⚙) → Let people find me**.

**Your profile** lists the loved ones, isms and stories you share with whoever is looking. Your friends and the people in your groups can open it, and see your name on what you share. Everyone else sees "Shared by their family" on what you share with everyone, and can't open your profile, unless you turn on **Settings (⚙) → Public profile**: then anyone signed in who can see something you shared also sees your name on it, and can open your profile. Either way, your name shows with your own comments and likes, including any you leave on your own loved ones. Your email address is never shown to other people.

**Keeping shared memories kind.** Shared isms, stories, loved ones and comments whose words might hurt, accuse or embarrass someone are held until a reviewer at Isms looks at them, and so is anything three people report. Until then, only the person who wrote it can see it. Reviewers can read what is held or reported so they can decide whether to allow or remove it. They never see anything you keep to yourself.

## How we use it

We use this information only to run Isms: to sign you in, to save what you add, to help you find and connect with friends and groups, to keep shared memories kind, and to show what you add to you (and, for loved ones you choose to share, to your friends, your group or other people using Isms, along with the likes and comments on them). When you add a loved one, the app checks whether someone with the same name and dates or obituary is already shared with you, so it can ask "Is this who you're looking for?". That check only looks at loved ones you can already see. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, the database, file storage and sign-in service Isms runs on. Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Settings (⚙) → Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your account, your name and friend list, the groups you made, your likes, comments and reports, and every loved one, photo, ism and story you added, including the ones you added to other people's loved ones. Loved ones other people shared with a group you made become private again for them. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing vipbdiddy@gmail.com.
