# Ism's Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Ism's collects, how it is used, and who can see it.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address and a protected (hashed) version of your password.
- **What you add about your loved ones.** Their names, relationships, years, short descriptions, obituaries and photos, and the ism's (quotes) and stories you save, along with the topics you tag them with.
- **Your saved favorites.** Which ism's and stories you have marked with ♥.
- **Your name and friends.** The name you choose for your friends to see (if you sign in with Google, it starts as your Google name), your friend code, and who you are friends with or have sent or received friend requests from.
- **On your phone only.** Your display preferences, and a name you enter before signing in, stay on your device.

## Who can see it

Everything you add is private by default: only you can see it.

For each loved one, you choose who can see their name, relationship, years, description, obituary, photo, ism's and stories:

- **Just me** (the default): only you.
- **Friends**: you and the people you have added as friends in Ism's.
- **Everyone**: everyone who uses Ism's, including people who are not signed in.

You can keep individual ism's or stories private, and you can change who can see a loved one at any time, which takes effect right away.

**Your name** (the one you choose for friends) is shown to your friends, to people you send a friend request to, and to people who send you one. People who are not your friends never see which loved ones are yours. Your **friend code** is only shared when you share it. Your email address is never shown to other people.

## How we use it

We use this information only to run Ism's: to sign you in, to save what you add, to connect you with the friends you add, and to show what you add to you (and, for loved ones you choose to share, to your friends or to other people using Ism's). When you add a loved one, the app checks whether someone with the same name and dates or obituary is already shared with you, so it can ask "Is this who you're looking for?". That check only looks at loved ones you can already see. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, the database, file storage and sign-in service Ism's runs on. Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Settings (⚙) → Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your account, your name, friend code and friend list, and every loved one, photo, ism and story you added. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing vipbdiddy@gmail.com.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
