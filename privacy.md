# Ism's Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Ism's collects and how it is used.

## What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We also store the ism's and any notes you choose to save.

## How we use it

We use this information only to sign you in and to show you the ism's you have saved. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, the database and sign-in service Ism's runs on.

## Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything you have saved at any time by emailing vipbdiddy@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
