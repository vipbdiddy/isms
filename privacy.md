# Ism's Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Ism's collects, how it is used, and who can see it.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign up with an email address, we store that address and a protected (hashed) version of your password.
- **What you add about your loved ones.** Their names, relationships, years, short descriptions, obituaries and photos, and the ism's (quotes) and stories you save, along with the topics you tag them with.
- **Your saved favorites.** Which ism's and stories you have marked with ♥.
- **On your phone only.** The first name you enter for the greeting and your display preferences stay on your device.

## Who can see it

Everything you add is private by default: only you can see it.

If you turn on **"Share their memories with everyone"** for a loved one, their name, relationship, years, description, obituary, photo, ism's and stories become visible to everyone who uses Ism's, including people who are not signed in. You can keep individual ism's or stories private, and you can turn sharing off at any time, which hides them again right away. Your own name and email address are never shown to other people.

## How we use it

We use this information only to run Ism's: to sign you in, to save what you add, and to show it to you (and, for loved ones you choose to share, to other people using Ism's). We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Where it is stored

Your information is stored with Supabase, the database, file storage and sign-in service Ism's runs on. Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: **Settings → Delete my account**. This permanently deletes your account and every loved one, photo, ism and story you added. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing vipbdiddy@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
