# Help keep Isms free

Isms is free, with no ads, and it will stay that way. Running it still costs a little: the hosting, database and storage that keep everyone's memories safe and the app available.

If you'd like to help, a donation goes toward those costs and keeps Isms available for every family. It's never required, and everything in the app works the same whether you give or not.

**Online donations are coming soon.** Until then, if you'd like to help, email [vipbdiddy@gmail.com](mailto:vipbdiddy@gmail.com). Sharing Isms with someone who would love it helps too.

Thank you.
