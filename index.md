<section class="why" aria-labelledby="why-isms">

  <h2 class="why-label" id="why-isms">Why Isms</h2>
  <div class="why-mark" aria-hidden="true">“</div>
  <p>Isms started after my friend Bryce died unexpectedly and left behind seven young kids. I built it so they could still hear his voice — through the stories and advice people who knew him share. When they’re older and facing something hard, they can look up what their dad would have said.</p>
  <img class="why-swoosh" src="assets/logo-swoosh.png" alt="">
</section>

<section class="intro">
  <p>Every family has them. The sayings, phrases and little expressions that made someone who they were. Isms gives you one place to keep them, so they are never forgotten.</p>
  <p>Sign in with your Google account or an email address to start saving. Everything is private unless you choose to share it: with your friends on Isms, with a group like your family, or with everyone. The people you share with can add their own stories and sayings, like what you shared, and leave a kind word.</p>
</section>
