# Ism's

A place to save and remember ism's from friends and family who have passed on.

Every family has them. The sayings, phrases and little expressions that made someone who they were. Ism's gives you one place to keep them, so they are never forgotten.

Sign in with your Google account or an email address to start saving. Everything is private unless you choose to share it: with your friends on Ism's, with a group like your family, or with everyone. The people you share with can like what you shared and leave a kind word.

**[Download Ism's for Android](https://github.com/vipbdiddy/isms/releases/latest/download/isms.apk)**

**On an iPhone, or any computer: [open Ism's in your browser](app/).** On an iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**, and it opens like an app.
