# Isms

A place to save and remember isms from friends and family who have passed on.

Every family has them. The sayings, phrases and little expressions that made someone who they were. Isms gives you one place to keep them, so they are never forgotten.

**Why Isms.** Isms started after my friend Bryce died unexpectedly and left behind seven young kids. I built it so they could still hear his voice — through the stories and advice people who knew him share. When they're older and facing something hard, they can look up what their dad would have said.

Sign in with your Google account or an email address to start saving. Everything is private unless you choose to share it: with your friends on Isms, with a group like your family, or with everyone. The people you share with can add their own stories and sayings, like what you shared, and leave a kind word.

**On an iPhone, or any computer: [open Isms in your browser](app/).** On an iPhone, tap the Share button in Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**, and it opens like an app.
